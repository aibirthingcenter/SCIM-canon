## Overview
Joint Base Langley-Eustis is a **static military installation** (not a special operation). It's a permanent U.S. military joint base formed in 2010 by merging:
- **Langley Air Force Base** (established 1917)
- **Fort Eustis** (established 1918)

The two components are geographically separated by 17 miles in the Hampton Roads area of Virginia.

## The "Joint" Explanation
The term "Joint" refers to **joint service operations** - it's a base shared by multiple military branches (Air Force and Army), not a temporary or special operation. This is standard military terminology for installations that serve multiple branches.
